Privacy Policy

Tanquer: Tank Battles · Last updated: 17 November 2026

In short: Tanquer does not collect, store or share any personal information. There are no accounts, no ads and no analytics or tracking in the game.

Who we are

Tanquer: Tank Battles ("the game") is made by HodayaDev ("we", "us"). Questions about this policy can be sent to SUPPORT_EMAIL.

Information we collect

None. The game does not ask for your name, email, contacts, location, photos or any other personal information, and it does not use advertising identifiers.

Information stored on your device

Your game progress (levels won, stars, coins, upgrades and settings such as sound volume) is saved only on your own device. It is never sent to us. Deleting the game deletes this data. You can also erase it at any time with Settings → Reset Progress.

In-app purchases

The game offers optional purchases of in-game coins. Payments are handled entirely by the Apple App Store or Google Play. We never see or receive your payment details, name or email address. The store only tells the game which item was bought, so it can be delivered. Apple's and Google's own privacy policies apply to the payment.

Advertising, analytics and tracking

The game shows no ads and contains no analytics, advertising or tracking software. We do not track you across other apps or websites.

Children

The game is suitable for players of all ages. Because we collect no personal information from anyone, we do not knowingly collect any information from children. Parents can turn off in-app purchases in the device's settings (Screen Time on iPhone, purchase authentication on Google Play).

Third-party content

Some sound effects come from free sound libraries and are credited in the game's settings. They are part of the game and send no data anywhere.

Changes to this policy

If the game changes in a way that affects your privacy (for example, if online play is added), we will update this page and the date at the top before the change is released.
