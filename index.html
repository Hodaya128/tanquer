Frequently asked questions

I bought coins but didn't get them.

Close the game completely and open it again: purchases that were interrupted are delivered when the game starts. If the coins still don't appear, email us with the date of the purchase and the store receipt (from Apple or Google), and we will sort it out.

How do I get a refund?

Refunds are handled by the store you bought from. On iPhone, go to reportaproblem.apple.com. On Android, open Google Play → Profile → Payments & subscriptions → Budget & history.

Is my progress saved? Can I move it to a new phone?

Progress is saved on your device. The game has no accounts, so progress can't be moved to another device yet. Deleting the game also deletes your progress.

How do I start over?

Open Settings → Reset Progress. This erases all stars, coins, XP and upgrades and cannot be undone.

The game is too hard or too easy.

Upgrade your tanks in the garage with coins, try a different army or abilities, and use the free revive (FIX) on fallen tanks. We read every message about difficulty, so tell us which level and we will look at it.

Is there online play?

Not yet. Online battles are planned for a future update.

Sound or music is too loud or too quiet.

Open Settings and use the SOUND FX and MUSIC sliders.
